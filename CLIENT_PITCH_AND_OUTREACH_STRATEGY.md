## 1. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

1. **Local Authority Angle:** Aap Bharatpur se hi hain. Ek local developer ka trust hamesha bahar ke kisi agency se 10x zyada hota hai.
2. **Effort-First Proof:** 99% log cold message me kehte hain *"Sir website banwani hai kya?"* (Spam lagta hai). Aap unhe bolte hain: *"Maine aapke hospital ke liye already ek tailored working demo live bana diya hai, aap bas 1 minute nikaal kar dekh lijiye."*
3. **Loss-Aversion / Gap Highlight:** Bharatpur ke log jab emergency me fracture ya trauma doctor search karte hain, unhe verified information, direct doctor profile aur 1-tap call chahiye hoti hai. Agar ye nahi hai, toh patient direct Mathura, Agra ya Jaipur refer sochta hai ya doosre clinic chala jata hai.
4. **Zero-Friction CTA:** Pehle message me koi quotation ya pushy sales pitch nahi. Sirf consent maangna: *"Kya main aapko iska 1-minute live link share kar sakta hoon?"*
